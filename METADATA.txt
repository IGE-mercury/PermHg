Lake Sediment Data Column Descriptions:

Variable	Units	        Description
Core_ID		(-)             Unique sample identifier: Concatenation of Site_ID, Location_ID, Latitute, and Longitude
Site_ID	        (-)	        Site name or code as defined in the reference paper
Location_ID	(-)	        State, province, or region of sample, as defined in the reference paper
Country		(-)             Country of sample
Sample_ID	(-)	        Sample number or code as defined in the reference paper
Date	        (mm/dd/yyyy)	Date the sample was collected
Laboratory	(-)	        Name of laboratory or institution where samples were processed
Contact_Name	(-)	        Name of person who collected, processed, or published the sample data
Contact_email	(-)	        Email for contact
paper_code	(-)	        First author Last name _ Publication year e.g., Lim_2020
Latitude	(deg)	        Latitude of sample
Longitude	(deg)	        Longitude of sample
Soil_typ	(-)	        Soil type
Horizon	        (-)	        Soil horizon of sample
Veg_typ	        (-)	        Vegetation type
D_min	        (cm)	        Depth to top of sample
D_max	        (cm)	        Depth to bottom of sample
D_ave	        (cm)	        Average depth of sample
Bulk_den	(g/cm3)	        Bulk soil density
Bulk_den_err	(g/cm3)	        Uncertainty bulk soil density
LOI	        (Fraction)	Mass Loss on Ignition
SOC_inst	(-)	        Technique used to calculate SOC
SOC	        (%)	        Soil Organic Carbon, ratio of carbon to total soil mass
SOC_err	        (%)	        Uncertainty Soil Organic Carbon, ratio of carbon to total soil mass
STHg_inst	(-)	        Instrument or technique to measure STHg
STHg	        (ng Hg/g soil)	Sediment Total mercury, ratio of Hg mass to total soil mass
STHg_err	(ng Hg/g soil)	Uncertainty Sediment Total mercury, ratio of Hg mass to total soil mass
RHgC	        (µg Hg/g C)	Mercury to carbon ratio
RHgC_err	(µg Hg/g C)	Uncertainty Mercury to carbon ratio
VWC	        (-)	        Volumetric Water Content
VWC_err	        (-)	        Uncertainty Volumetric Water Content
Notes	        (-)	        Notes from extractor regarding data. To avoid long repeated texts in this column we have created 3 identifiers which refer to following descriptions:
    Note1: for depth average, put in the average depth of TOC and Hg from digitizations since on separate plots but should be matching depths

    Note2: impossible to tell where TOC points were during digitization; for depth average, put in the average depth of TOC and Hg from digitizations since on separate plots but should be matching depths

    Note3: Database compiled by M. Nasr, used in Nasr (2017)



Collector	(-)	        Initials of extractor

Aquatic/Water Data Column Descriptions:

Core_ID	        (-)	        Unique sample identifier: Concatenation of Site_ID, Location_ID, Latitute, and Longitude
Site_ID	        (-)	        Site name or code as defined in the reference paper
Location_ID	(-)	        State, province, or region of sample, as defined in the reference paper
Sample_ID	(-)	        Sample number or code as defined in the reference paper
Date	        (mm/dd/yyyy)	Date the sample was collected in month/day/year format; use 'x' for missing information
Laboratory	(-)	        Name of laboratory or institution where samples were processed
Contact_name	(-)	        Name of person who collected, processed, or published the sample data
Contact_email	(-)	        Email for contact
Paper_code	(-)	        First author Last name _ Publication year e.g., Lim_2020
Latitude	(deg)	        Latitude of sample
Longitude	(deg)	        Longitude of sample
Soil_type	(-)	        Soil type near sampling location
Veg_type	(-)	        Vegetation type near sampling location
Sample_location	(-)	        Description of water body sample was collected from
PW_soil_descrip	(-)	        Description of soil where porewater sample was taken, if applicable
Catchment_size	(ha)	        Size of catchment in hectares, if applicable
Wetland_cover	(%)	        Percent wetland cover, if applicable
Depth_sample	(cm)	        Depth of water sample from surface
THg	        (ng/L)	        Total mercury, nanogram per liter
THg_err	        (ng/L)	        Total mercury, measured uncertainty or error
DHg	        (ng/L)	        Dissolved mercury, nanogram per liter
DHg_err	        (ng/L)	        Dissolved mercury, measured uncertainty or error
DHg_filter	(µm)	        Dissolved mercury filter pore size, micrometers 
MeHg	        (ng/L)	        Methyl mercury, nanogram per liter
MeHg_err	(ng/L)	        Methyl mercury, measured uncertainty or error
TOC	        (mg/L)	        Total organic carbon, milligram per liter as carbon
TOC_err	        (mg/L)	        Total organic carbon, measured uncertainty or error
DOC	        (mg/L)	        Dissolved organic carbon, milligram per liter
DOC_err	        (mg/L)	        Dissolved organic carbon, measured uncertainty or error
DOC_filter	(µm)	        Dissolved organic carbon filter pore size, micrometers
RHgC	        (µg Hg/g C)	Mercury (THg) to carbon ratio
Turbidity	(NTU)	        Turbidity, Nephelometric Turbidity Units 
pH	        (SU)	        pH of sample
EC	        (S/m)	        Electrical conductivity, measured in Siemens per meter
Alk	        (mg/L as CaCO3)	Alkalinity, milligrams per liter as calcium carbonate
P	        (ug/L)	        Phosphorus, milligrams per liter
Cl	        (mg/L)	        Chloride, milligrams per liter
SO4	        (mg/L) 	        Sulfate, milligrams per liter
TDS	        (mg/L)	        Total dissolved solids, milligrams per liter
Notes	        (-)	        Notes from collector regarding data
Collector	(-)	        Initials of sample collector
